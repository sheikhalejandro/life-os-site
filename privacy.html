Privacy policy

Life OS is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • It accesses Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets) only for accounts belonging to its owner, and only with read-only permission unless the owner grants more.
  • Data is processed and stored on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
